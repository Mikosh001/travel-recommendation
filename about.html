HELLO, WE’RE TRAVELBLOOM

Good places.
Thoughtful discoveries.

TravelBloom is a student-built travel recommendation project. Our purpose is to help curious travelers explore beaches, temples and countries through clear descriptions and inspiring photography.

Search by category or destination and discover somewhere that matches your interests. Our collection is intentionally small, making it easy to compare possibilities before planning your own trip.

We value curiosity, respect for local cultures, accessible design and honest information. This educational project offers inspiration; it does not sell bookings or tours.

HOW WE WORK

Guidance for
team members.

Use this checklist when contributing to TravelBloom.

  1. Research with care. Confirm destination names and facts using official tourism or heritage sources.
  2. Respect every place. Write original, inclusive descriptions and represent local cultures thoughtfully.
  3. Credit your images. Use properly licensed photos, descriptive alt text and an entry on the credits page.
  4. Check the experience. Test singular, plural and mixed-case searches; verify Clear, navigation, the contact form and mobile layouts.
  5. Review before publishing. Keep changes on a branch, review the code and never commit passwords or private information.
